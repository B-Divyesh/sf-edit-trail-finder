Policy / plain language

Privacy

Effective 27 August 2026

Edit Trail is designed so your photo archive stays yours. The CLI runs locally. It reads supported sidecar metadata and writes an index on your computer. It does not read image pixels, upload files, call analytics services, or transmit your index.

What the CLI stores

The local index contains sidecar paths, inferred source-image paths, modification timestamps, editor family, active operation names, and parse warnings. You choose its location and may delete it at any time.

Browser demo

Sidecars selected or pasted into the browser demo are processed in browser memory. Their contents are not sent to our servers and disappear when the tab closes. The site uses a service worker cache so documentation and the demo can reopen offline.

Analytics and third parties

There are no analytics, advertising pixels, third-party fonts, runtime CDN scripts, accounts, or payment requests.

Your control

Clear this site’s storage to remove offline files. Delete .edit-trail.json to remove a CLI index. For privacy questions, open an issue in the public project repository without including private paths.
